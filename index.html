Afsana Khan

Federated Learning | Privacy-Preserving ML | Multimodal Learning

I am a postdoctoral researcher working on federated learning: training machine learning models across organizations without sharing their raw data. I focus on vertical federated learning, where different parties hold different features about the same individuals.

I completed my PhD in the Department of Advanced Computing Sciences at Maastricht University in 2026, supervised by Prof. Anna Wilbik. My dissertation, Unlocking value of Data with Vertical Federated Learning, covers communication-efficient training, secure participant selection, and fair incentive allocation. I applied these ideas to financial crime detection with SWIFT data.

Most recently I worked on machine health monitoring in the DigiMach Interreg project, predicting tool wear from sensor and image data with deep learning and federated training across metal industry partners.

Before that, I earned an M.Sc. in Computer Science (Distributed Systems) at the University of Tartu, and a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at the Bangladesh University of Professionals.
